How to use Passport Size Photo Maker & Print Sheet

  1. 1. Upload a clear front-facing photo from your device.
  2. 2. Choose your passport size (35×45 mm, 2×2 in...) and crop the face.
  3. 3. Pick your paper sheet size (A4 or 4×6), copy count, and gap.
  4. 4. Click Generate Sheet and download JPG or PDF to print at 100%.
